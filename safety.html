SAFETY FIRST

Keep every exchange safe and free

Every item is listed for $0. Members should arrange safe collection directly and report anything suspicious.

🛡️

Zero payment requests. Zero exceptions.

Listings cannot ask for money, shipping charges, deposits, swaps for cash or handling fees. Report anything suspicious. Free-item giveaways usually use local pickup. Wanted requests can arrange free pickup or free delivery, but no cash, paid shipping or delivery fees.

📍 Choose a safe place

Meet in a public, well-lit location when possible. Let a trusted person know your plans and avoid going alone if you are unsure.

🔍 Check the item

Read the description and inspect condition at collection. Ask about damage or missing parts before arranging pickup.

💬 Keep communication clear

Agree on collection time, place and what is included. Don't share unnecessary sensitive personal information.

🚩 Report concerns

If someone requests payment, sends suspicious links or behaves inappropriately, do not proceed. Report the listing to the site administrator.
